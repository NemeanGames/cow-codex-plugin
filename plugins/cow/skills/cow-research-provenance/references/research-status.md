# COW research-status guardrails

Historical pilot materials used the earlier name **Continuity Workflow Ontology**. The current product name is **Continuity Of Work Ontology (COW)**.

The measured pilot results are instrumentation results, not a verified general efficiency claim. The catalog cohorts had five matched pairs each; Test C had three pairs. The final catalog configuration had a 1.076x assisted/baseline overhead (7.6% aggregate premium), with three of five pairs within 0.5% of context-token parity at identical request counts. Test C observed a 2.967 baseline/assisted context-token ratio (66.3% fewer context tokens in the assisted arm) but missed its registered 3x threshold.

Audit 5 ended the declared audit cycle at 49 PASS, 0 FAIL, 3 UNKNOWN, disposition INDETERMINATE. R31, R39, and R41 remained UNKNOWN. Do not promote these results into monetary savings, broad reliability gains, live-editor qualification, or cross-domain claims.
